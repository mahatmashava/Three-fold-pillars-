I. The Esoteric Foundation of the Three Pillars
This system utilizes three distinct colors to ground the divination in specific energetic frequencies. Each color represents a different aspect of your timeline:

Red (The Blood Root): Represents the Past. It symbolizes vitality, previous struggles, and the "blood" of your history that continues to fuel your present self.

White (The Albedo Path): Represents the Present. It symbolizes purification, clarity, and the "white light" of current awareness and immediate action.

Yellow (The Solar Fruit): Represents the Future. It symbolizes enlightenment, the "inner sun," and the ultimate spiritual harvest yet to come.
